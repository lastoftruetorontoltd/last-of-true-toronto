# Last of True Toronto, Ltd.
### Toronto’s Industrial Identity • Niagara Hydroelectric History • Foundational Infrastructure Research

**Last of True Toronto, Ltd.** is a research and documentation project dedicated to recovering Toronto’s authentic industrial identity through the history of **Niagara Falls hydroelectric power**, public-power development, and the engineering systems that shaped the city’s growth.

This repository serves as the **public knowledge archive** for the master’s thesis project *Last of True Toronto*, exploring how foundational energy systems—beginning with the ancient water wheel and culminating in the Sir Adam Beck Generating Stations—constructed the material, cultural, and infrastructural DNA of Toronto.

---

## 📘 Project Purpose
Toronto’s modern identity is often framed through finance, real estate, and global branding.  
This project argues that the **true Toronto** is rooted in:

- **Niagara hydroelectric power (1906–present)**  
- **Public power under Sir Adam Beck**  
- **Industrial electrification and street railways**  
- **Foundational infrastructure systems**  
- **Engineering megaprojects that shaped Ontario’s energy independence**

This repository documents the research, archival findings, and analytical frameworks supporting the thesis.

---

## 🏛️ Research Themes
- Hydroelectric megaprojects (Queenston–Chippawa, Sir Adam Beck I & II)  
- Early private syndicate power (Toronto Power Generating Station, 1906)  
- Public power politics (Hydro-Electric Power Commission of Ontario)  
- Urban identity formation through infrastructure  
- Water wheel → hydroelectric lineage  
- Industrial heritage and cultural memory
